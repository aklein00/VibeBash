# Make-Ads

Index note. The output is an ad brief appended to [[campaigns]], not a live ad.

1. Read the current section of [[campaigns]]. If it is missing or "not approved," write the brief and mark it blocked.
2. Read [[offer]] and [[audience]].
3. Read [[scoreboard]]. Do not claim a member count or a revenue number that is still a placeholder.
4. Write three ad concepts in the campaign section: the line, who it is for, what they click, what the landing page must repeat.
5. The landing page must repeat the price and the trial from [[offer]].
6. Flag any claim that is not in [[Fieldnote]] or [[offer]].
7. Do not publish, and do not spend. This job has no ad-account access.
8. Append one index line to today's daily note.
